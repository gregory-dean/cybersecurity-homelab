# Mounts

Everything in the rack that is printed is black PETG. I did not design the Tiny trays or the patch panel. The switch tray is mine. The Printables pages are the source.

## Tiny PC trays (1U)

Used for Sirius, Polaris, and Vega.

- Model: [10 Inch Rack Mount for Lenovo Thinkcentre m920q (m720q) Tiny](https://www.printables.com/model/1384009-10-inch-rack-mount-for-lenovo-thinkcentre-m920q-m7)
- Designer: owlish
- Origin: remix of Smelliot's M900 Tiny mount, feet channels moved for the m920q and m720q footprint. The M715q (Vega) uses the same tray.
- Print: faceplate down, 3 perimeters at 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.25 mm layers, PETG. About 130 g each.
- Post process: clip the sacrificial bridges so screws pass.
- Hardware: optional two M3x6 screws into the Tiny. The rubber feet also hold it.

## Patch panel (0.5U)

- Model: [MiniRack 10-inch Blank Keystone Patch Panel](https://www.printables.com/model/1316293-minirack-10-inch-blank-keystone-patch-panel)
- Designer: Juraj
- Variant: 12 port
- Print: 0.2 mm layers, support free, black PETG
- Jacks: Cat6A keystone couplers from the 25 pack

## Switch tray (1U)

Personal design for the LS108GP.

## Rack

GeeekPi / DeskPi RackMate T1, 8U, 10 inch. Switch tray on top, 0.5U panel under it, then Sirius, a 1U blank, Polaris, a 1U blank, Vega, then a 1.5U blank at the bottom.
